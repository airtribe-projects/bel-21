### LLD (50 minutes)
- Crammed solution never work
- Structured approach
- Not to come up with a working solution but engage in *Meaningful dialogue*


### Stages (4 stage approach)

#### Requirement Gathering (5-10 minutes)
    - Try to explore the problem statment from all th different angles.
    - Ask clarifying questions
    - Assumptions: State those assumptions upfront and agree with the interviewer
    - Confirm with the interviewer before proceeding
    - Functional 
    - Non functional requirement
        - Modular
        - Scalable
        - Low Coupling
        - Fast 

#### Identifying the entities of the system (5 minutes)
    - Identify the nouns of the system
    - Generally it would be close to 10-15
    - Correlate it with the requirement 
    - Thinking through the major flows of the system


#### Detail Design of the system (30 minutes)
    - Go Entity by entity
    - Identify the attributes of the entity
    - Identify the behavious of the entity
    - Identify the relationships of the entity

    - Where to start? 
        - Top to Bottom Approach
            - Start with the largest / most complex entity
        - Bottom to Top Approach
            - Start with the smallest / least complex / simplest entity and then build around it

#### Diagram (5 minutes)
    - Class Diagram
    - Pen / Paper
    - draw.io

#### Follow up questions (5-10)
    - Cross question on approach
    - Extend the design to accomodate a new use case
    - To write code for specific behaviour or a specific flow (uncommon)
